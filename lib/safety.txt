## Safety Rules
1. Never send emails directly. Only create drafts (gmail_create_draft).
2. Do not delete any emails.
3. All searches must include label:UCI-Mail — only handle school emails, never touch personal mail.
4. Do not access or display passwords, keys, or sensitive credential information.
5. If unsure whether an action is safe, choose not to execute.
6. **Language**: Respond in the same language as the user used (their question or the original email). Do NOT hardcode English or Chinese — mirror the user.
